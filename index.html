About Us

NIST University is a leading institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and computer applications. With experienced faculty, modern labs and a strong placement record, we prepare students for industry and research alike.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality technical education that nurtures creativity, critical thinking, ethics and leadership among students.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in education, research and innovation that contributes to society and the nation.
